Fares — Town of Falmouth tariff

Meter rates
Approved by the Falmouth Select Board (Jan 9, 2023):
• Flag / minimum: $3.00
• $0.75 per 1/5 mile ($3.75/mile)
• Time in transit: $0.45/min, in addition to mileage
• Waiting: $26/hr after 3 free minutes at pickup
• Trunk items: $0.50 each (hand bags free)
The on-screen estimate uses distance only ($3.00 + $3.75/mile); your final fare is by the meter.
Senior & disability discount
The Town tariff provides a 25% discount for holders of a Council on Aging senior card, MassRehab card, or MCB card. Present your card to the driver for the Town 25% discount; estimate shown is the full meter rate.
Out of town
Trips leaving Falmouth are on the regular meter. No flat rates are permitted.

Service

Area & hours
Falmouth & East Falmouth (demo), including the Woods Hole ferry terminal. Falmouth taxicab companies must operate 24/7 under town rules.
Extra stops
Yes — tap “＋ Add stop” (up to 3). The fare continues on the meter through each stop; there is no separate stop fee.
Live map
The live driver map unlocks about 5 minutes before pickup.
Payment & updates
Choose cash or card. Card readers are planned. Opt in to SMS trip updates (demo).
